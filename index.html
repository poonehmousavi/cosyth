Do these belong together?

On each page you get either a sound or a spoken sentence, plus a few candidates of the other kind. We want to know whether the two are related: could they happen together, in the same situation? Give each candidate one score from 0 to 100.

Ask yourself

1
Same situation?Could this sound and this sentence happen in the same moment? The sentence need not describe the sound.
2
Right amount of detail?Not vague, and nothing that doesn't belong.
3
Really speech, or really a sound?Not silence, noise, or the wrong kind of clip.
4
Clean and natural?No glitches or robotic artifacts.
5
Emotion, pace and mood fit?For the voice and for the background alike.
BadPoorFairGoodExcellent
Please use headphones in a quiet room. The test takes about 14 minutes. Use the whole range, and equal scores are fine. Your answers are anonymous.

High score

  • Clear speech, natural sounding.

Low score
